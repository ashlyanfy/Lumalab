# Implemented improvement summary

## Positioning

The site now presents LumaLab as an equity growth partner for large companies with active digital products. The copy emphasizes measurable growth, safe NDA-based entry, product diagnostics, pilots and partnership structure.

## Investor and enterprise readiness

Added blocks that make the site stronger for investors and large companies:

- clear fit / not-fit criteria;
- safe entry process and NDA logic;
- own digital products under NDA;
- structured team capabilities;
- phased process from application to scale;
- richer company and talent forms.

## Careers

Vacancies were rewritten as living role cards with:

- what the person will do;
- who LumaLab is looking for;
- general candidate criteria;
- specialist application form.

## Design

The site was converted from dark tech style to a light, premium B2B style with clouds, digitalization, natural elements and thematic line icons.
